## Rugs for stairs ##

- They snap to the middle of the stairs, but they also have an option to snap together.
- They work perfectly with Marble, Grausten and modded stairs, but with stone stairs they sometimes glitch through, as the stone stairs vibrate when placed and change shape. I tried to match them as much as possible to the stone stairs, but even with all these efforts they still find a corner to glitch through.

## 💡 Multicolored Lamps & Light Colors

![New](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Caen007/MoonforgedBuildPieces/main/img/banner.png)

Atmospheric lighting with full color control.

### Features:

- Color cycle: Yellow → Green → Blue → Dvergr Pink → OFF
- Correct emission, flicker, and shadow behavior
- OFF = bulb turns fully black (no glow)

### 🔒 Light Ownership

- Only the player who placed the lamp can change the color.
- Visitors cannot alter or spam your lights.

## 🔨 How to Install

1. **Install these first:**
   - [BepInEx Pack](https://valheim.thunderstore.io/package/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/)
   - [Jotunn](https://valheim.thunderstore.io/package/ValheimModding/Jotunn/)

2. **Add this mod:**
   - Download the `.dll`
   - Put it in:
     `BepInEx/plugins/MoonforgedBuildPieces/`

3. **Launch Valheim:**
   - Your new build pieces will appear in the Hammer menu!
